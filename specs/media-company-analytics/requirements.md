## 1. Business Context and Objective

### About MediaCo

MediaCo is a UK public service broadcaster (PSB) established by Parliament in 1982. Unlike the BBC, MediaCo receives **no licence fee funding** — it is a publicly-owned, **commercially self-funded** broadcaster that derives **virtually all of its revenue from advertising sales** across its linear TV channels (MediaCo, EXTRA, MediaCo Factual, MediaCo Movies, MediaCo Catchup) and its digital streaming platform, **MediaCo Streaming** (now branded as MediaCo streaming, an AVOD service — advertising-funded video on demand).

MediaCo operates under a **statutory remit** set by the Communications Act 2003 and is regulated by **Ofcom**. The remit requires the broadcaster to deliver content that:
- Appeals to the tastes and interests of a culturally diverse society
- Includes a substantial proportion of news, current affairs, and educational programming
- Stimulates well-informed debate and reflects the cultural diversity of the UK
- Includes high levels of original UK content

### Business priorities

| Priority | Description | Strategic owner |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Maximise ad revenue | Linear TV + AVOD (MediaCo Streaming) — sole revenue stream, must be optimised across dayparts, audiences, and advertisers | Ad Sales Director |
| Commission the right shows | Use viewership and engagement data to inform commissioning, scheduling, and acquisition decisions | Head of Scheduling, Commissioning |
| Grow and retain MediaCo Streaming audience | Streaming is the long-term growth lever as linear TV viewing declines | Digital Product Manager |
| Demonstrate PSB remit delivery | Reach and diversity reporting to Ofcom under the statutory licence | Ofcom Reporting Analyst |

### Why now

Linear TV audiences are declining year-over-year (industry trend across all UK PSBs), while MediaCo Streaming streaming consumption is growing. MediaCo needs a **unified analytics layer** that reconciles linear viewership (BARB) with digital event-level data (MediaCo Streaming) so that decisions about commissioning, scheduling, and ad inventory pricing can be made on **total audience** rather than channel-by-channel silos. Today these data sources live in disconnected systems and analysts produce reports manually, leading to inconsistent KPIs across teams (Ad Sales says one CPM, Programming sees another).

### Objective

Build a **dbt analytics layer** that:
- Centralises linear TV ratings, ad sales, MediaCo Streaming streaming, and audience demographic data into a single warehouse model
- Defines canonical metrics (TVR, consolidated 7-day reach, CPM, MAU, completion rate, share of viewing) once, used everywhere
- Powers dashboards for Ad Sales, Scheduling, Digital, and Ofcom reporting
- Exposes metrics through the dbt Semantic Layer for analyst self-service
- Refreshes daily with overnight BARB data and hourly MediaCo Streaming event data

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (EARS)

### CA-01: TVR calculation
- **Event:** When a viewer figure is computed for a programme on a channel
- **Action:** The system calculates TVR as `(viewers_thousands / uk_population_thousands) * 100`
- **Result:** TVR is expressed as a percentage of the UK 4+ population (≈63,000 thousand)
- **State:** The UK population baseline is sourced from a versioned seed (`uk_population_baseline.csv`), not hardcoded

### CA-02: Consolidated 7-day (C7) viewers
- **Event:** When C7 viewers are reported for a programme
- **Action:** The system uses `c7_viewers_thousands` from `barb_consolidated_audience` once available (≈8 days after first transmission)
- **Result:** Reports between TX+1 and TX+7 days show overnight only; reports from TX+8 onwards show C7
- **State:** A flag `is_c7_consolidated` marks each row as overnight-only or fully consolidated

### CA-03: Share of viewing
- **Event:** When share of viewing is requested for a channel and time window
- **Action:** The system calculates `channel_viewing_minutes / total_uk_tv_viewing_minutes_in_window`
- **Result:** Returns a percentage where the sum of all measured channels in the window is ≤ 100% (the remainder is "other channels")
- **State:** The competitor universe (BBC1, ITV1, Sky, etc.) is defined in `dim_channel`

### CA-04: Reach (15 consecutive minutes)
- **Event:** When Ofcom reach is calculated for a channel and period
- **Action:** The system counts distinct viewers who watched ≥ 15 consecutive minutes on that channel in the period
- **Result:** Reach is reported as both absolute (thousands of viewers) and as a percentage of the UK 4+ population
- **State:** Demographic breakdowns (16-34, ABC1, C2DE, gender) are available for every reach figure

### CA-05: Total revenue (linear + AVOD)
- **Event:** When total ad revenue is requested for an advertiser or campaign
- **Action:** The system sums `actual_revenue_gbp` from linear spots **and** `revenue_gbp` from AVOD impressions for the matching campaign IDs
- **Result:** Revenue is broken down by `platform` ('linear' | 'avod') so the split is auditable
- **State:** All revenue is in GBP; no FX conversion is required (UK-only sales)

### CA-06: CPM calculation
- **Event:** When CPM is requested for a daypart, channel, or campaign
- **Action:** The system calculates `(total_revenue_gbp / total_impressions) * 1000`
- **Result:** CPM is expressed as GBP per thousand impressions, rounded to 2 decimals
- **State:** Impressions and revenue must come from the same grain (a spot-level CPM is not aggregated from already-aggregated rows)

### CA-07: Spot delivery vs. booking
- **Event:** When a daily under-delivery report is produced
- **Action:** The system calculates `delivered_impressions_thousands / booked_impressions_thousands` per spot and flags spots below 95%
- **Result:** Under-delivered spots are listed with the campaign, advertiser, and impression shortfall
- **State:** The 95% threshold is configurable via a dbt variable (`var('underdelivery_threshold')`)

### CA-08: MediaCo Streaming MAU / WAU / DAU
- **Event:** When digital active user metrics are requested
- **Action:** The system counts distinct `user_id`s with at least one play event in the rolling window (1 / 7 / 30 days)
- **Result:** MAU is a rolling 30-day distinct count, not a calendar-month count, unless explicitly requested with the `calendar_mau` variant
- **State:** Anonymous (logged-out) users are counted via `session_id` and reported separately from registered users

### CA-09: Video completion rate
- **Event:** When completion rate is requested for a programme on MediaCo Streaming
- **Action:** The system calculates `play_events_with_event_type='complete' / play_events_with_event_type='start'`
- **Result:** Completion rate is between 0 and 1, expressed as a percentage in BI tools
- **State:** "Complete" is defined as the 100% progress event; shows shorter than 5 minutes are excluded to avoid skew from trailers/promos

### CA-10: Demographic breakdowns
- **Event:** When any audience metric is requested with a demographic filter
- **Action:** The system joins to `dim_demographic` and exposes age band, gender, and social grade as filter dimensions
- **Result:** Every audience metric (TVR, reach, MAU) can be sliced by any combination of demographic attributes
- **State:** The demographic taxonomy follows BARB conventions (4-15, 16-34, 35-54, 55+; M/F; ABC1/C2DE)

### CA-11: Semantic Layer exposure
- **Event:** When an analyst queries metrics through the dbt Semantic Layer
- **Action:** The metrics `tvr`, `share_of_viewing`, `reach_15min_consecutive`, `total_ad_revenue_gbp`, `cpm`, `streaming_mau`, `video_completion_rate` are all queryable
- **Result:** Each metric supports the dimensions defined in section 2b mapping
- **State:** Metric definitions in `semantic_models/` are the single source of truth — no duplicated logic in BI tools

### CA-12: Daily refresh SLA
- **Event:** When the production job runs overnight
- **Action:** The pipeline (seeds → staging → intermediate → marts → semantic) executes end-to-end
- **Result:** Total runtime ≤ 20 minutes; refreshed data is available before 07:00 UK time
- **State:** A freshness check on staging models fails the job if upstream seeds were not updated within the expected window

### CA-13: Broadcast calendar
- **Event:** When data is grouped by week or month
- **Action:** The system uses the **UK broadcast calendar** (broadcast weeks run Monday–Sunday; broadcast months follow the 4-4-5 convention used in UK media planning)
- **Result:** Weekly and monthly aggregates align with how BARB and the media industry report
- **State:** Both broadcast calendar and standard calendar columns are available in `dim_date` so analysts can choose

## 5. Out of Scope

- **Subscription / SVOD revenue** — MediaCo is AVOD-only; no Netflix-style subscriptions
- **International viewership** — UK-only audience, no international rights or distribution revenue
- **Predictive modelling** — no ML for audience forecasting, churn prediction, or content recommendation; this is descriptive analytics only
- **Real-time streaming analytics** — MediaCo Streaming events are batched (hourly minimum); no second-by-second live dashboards
- **Programmatic ad bidding logs** — only finalised ad insertions are modelled, not the upstream auction/bidding telemetry
- **Social media / sentiment data** — Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok engagement is not in scope
- **Content production cost data** — commissioning budgets and per-programme P&L are out of scope (ROI analysis would require this, marked as future work)
- **Pre-2020 historical data** — the demo focuses on recent broadcast years
- **BBC iPlayer / ITVX competitive streaming data** — only MediaCo's own MediaCo Streaming platform is modelled; linear competitor data is limited to BARB ratings
- **Ofcom-format regulatory submissions** — the system produces the underlying figures, but formatting into Ofcom's exact submission templates is handled downstream
- **PII handling beyond pseudonymous user IDs** — `user_id` is treated as opaque; no email, postcode, or identifiable demographic detail is stored

## 8. Glossary — UK Media Industry Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **PSB** | Public Service Broadcaster. UK PSBs are BBC, ITV, MediaCo, Channel 5, S4C. Subject to statutory remit and Ofcom regulation. |
| **BARB** | Broadcasters' Audience Research Board. The UK's official TV audience measurement body. Operates a representative panel of UK households and reports viewing minute-by-minute by demographic. The industry currency for TV ratings. |
| **TVR (Television Rating)** | Percentage of a target audience watching a programme at a given time. 1 TVR = 1% of the target audience (e.g. UK adults 16+). The base currency for TV ad sales. |
| **C7 (Consolidated 7-day)** | Audience figure including the live (overnight) broadcast plus any time-shifted playback within 7 days (recorded, catch-up, on-demand). The BARB standard for "total" audience. |
| **Overnight** | Live + same-day playback only — the figure available the morning after transmission. |
| **Reach** | Number (or %) of distinct individuals who watched a channel/programme for at least 15 consecutive minutes in a defined period. Used for Ofcom remit reporting. |
| **Share of viewing** | A channel's % of total TV viewing in a given period. MediaCo's portfolio share (MAIN + EXTRA + MediaCo Factual + MediaCo Movies + MediaCo Catchup) is typically 8–10%. |
| **BVOD** | Broadcaster Video On Demand. Streaming services run by broadcasters (MediaCo Streaming, BBC iPlayer, ITVX, My5). |
| **AVOD** | Advertising-funded Video On Demand. Free streaming supported by ads. MediaCo Streaming is AVOD. |
| **SVOD** | Subscription Video On Demand (Netflix, Disney+). MediaCo does NOT operate SVOD. |
| **MediaCo Streaming** | MediaCo's BVOD/AVOD platform (rebranded as "MediaCo streaming" but still commonly called MediaCo Streaming). |
| **CPM (Cost per Mille)** | Cost per thousand ad impressions. The standard ad pricing unit in the UK and globally. |
| **CPT (Cost per Thousand viewers)** | Similar to CPM but applied to programme viewers, used in linear TV planning. |
| **Daypart** | A defined block of the broadcast day used for ad pricing: breakfast (06:00–09:30), daytime (09:30–17:30), pre-peak (17:30–19:00), peak (19:00–22:30), post-peak (22:30–25:30), night (25:30–06:00). Peak attracts the highest CPMs. |
| **Solus spot** | An ad break containing only one advertiser's commercial — premium-priced. |
| **Make-good** | Free or discounted advertising offered to compensate when a campaign under-delivers its booked impressions. |
| **Spot** | A single ad airing on linear TV. |
| **Impression** | A single ad view (one viewer × one ad). 1,000 impressions = 1 CPM unit. |
| **EPG** | Electronic Programme Guide — the broadcast schedule shown to viewers. |
| **TX (Transmission)** | The broadcast of a programme. "First TX" = original linear airing. |
| **Ofcom** | The UK communications regulator. MediaCo reports reach and remit delivery to Ofcom under its statutory licence. |
| **Social grade** | UK demographic classification: A (upper middle), B (middle), C1 (lower middle), C2 (skilled working), D (working), E (subsistence). Usually aggregated to ABC1 / C2DE for ad targeting. |
| **16-34** | A key advertising demographic in the UK; harder to reach via linear TV, hence MediaCo Streaming's strategic importance. |
| **Broadcast calendar** | UK media planning uses Monday-start weeks and a 4-4-5 month convention (most months have 4 weeks; one month per quarter has 5) so that weeks align with months. |
| **Commissioning** | The process by which MediaCo buys or orders new programmes from production companies. MediaCo commissions but does not produce content in-house (the "publisher-broadcaster" model). |

## 9. Assumptions and Open Questions

### Assumptions
- BARB-style panel data is treated as authoritative for linear TV ratings (no second source)
- Seeds will represent **approximately 12 months** of broadcast data — enough to demonstrate YoY trends without making seed files unmanageably large
- The UK population baseline (≈63 million 4+) is fixed for the duration of the seed dataset
- MediaCo Streaming anonymous (logged-out) viewing is counted via session, not user
- Revenue figures are gross (before agency commission); net revenue modelling is out of scope
- The competitor channel universe is captured at the channel level only — no competitor programme-level ratings (we cannot model "BBC1 Strictly vs MAIN Bake Off" head-to-head with the seed data)
- All times are stored in UTC in fact tables and converted to Europe/London for reporting via `dim_date`

### Open Questions — RESOLVED

- [x] **Warehouse platform:** Snowflake (`zna84829`, `CCASTRO_SANDBOX`)
- [x] **AVOD revenue grain:** daily aggregate (1 row per programme × campaign × day) — not impression-level. Faster for demo; enables CPM and revenue reports.
- [x] **Simulcast handling:** count separately. Linear audience in `fct_programme_audience_daily`, streaming audience in `fct_streaming_session`. No cross-platform deduplication. Each row labelled by platform.
- [x] **CPM metrics:** both `rate_card_cpm_gbp` and `realised_cpm_gbp` modelled — enables yield/discount-depth analysis for the ad sales team.
- [ ] Are **MediaCo News** and other current affairs treated as a separate domain for remit reporting? (Assumed: rolled into general programme audience with `is_news_current_affairs` flag in `dim_programme`.)
- [ ] GDPR / demographic granularity constraints — assumed none for this demo; seeds use synthetic data only.
